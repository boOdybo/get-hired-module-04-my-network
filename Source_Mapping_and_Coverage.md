## 2. Source prompt preserved verbatim

```text
Write a LinkedIn headline for a fresh graduate in [field] targeting [sector]. Make it specific and value-focused.
```

## 3. Activity fidelity

The source's LinkedIn Makeover activity requires participants to:
1. Draft their LinkedIn Headline and About section using ChatGPT prompts.
2. Improve the drafts manually with personal details.
3. Receive trainer and peer review.
4. Read the best LinkedIn About section aloud; the peer-voted best section earns XP.

The deck retains these elements. The peer-review criteria shown in the deck are trainer-designed guidance, not official source scoring rules. No XP quantity is invented.

## 6. Additions and boundaries

The following are instructional additions, not verbatim source requirements:
- The three-question LinkedIn profile lens (Who are you? What can you contribute? What are you exploring?).
- The Headline formula: field/target role + relevant skills + value/direction.
- The five-part About structure: opening, focus, evidence, direction, invitation.
- Additional ChatGPT prompts for About, connection requests, and opportunity discovery.
- The verification checklist for AI-suggested opportunities.
- The five-criterion peer-review rubric.
- The 120-minute pacing distribution (the source states the 120-minute session window, not per-topic minutes).
- The Professional Profile Pack take-home assignment.

### Boundaries
- Do not fabricate qualifications, experience, skills, achievements, metrics, shared connections, or attendance at events.
- Do not claim a sample output is a real ChatGPT or Grammarly result unless it was actually generated and labelled.
- Do not assume Grammarly integrates with every LinkedIn text field.
- Do not present AI-generated groups or events as verified until their official pages and activity are checked.
- Do not assume LinkedIn AI profile suggestions are available to every student; some profile suggestion features are plan-dependent.
- Do not treat the other whole-day deliverables as direct AI Tools deliverables.
